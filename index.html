My Approach to Selling Your Home

STRAIGHT ANSWERS.
CLEAR PLAN.

When you're selling, you want to know exactly what you're getting. Here's how I work to get the best result for your property.

01
Will buyers really compete for my home?

Yes — when the right buyers find the right home at the right moment, they stretch. That's not luck. It's what happens when marketing is done properly. My job is to bring multiple qualified buyers to your property at the same time so they compete against each other — not against the asking price.

02
How do you know what my home is worth?

By looking at what buyers are actually paying — not what sellers hope to get. I use current comparable sales, active buyer demand, and real-time market data to give you an honest, defensible price range. Not a number designed to win the listing.

03
Where will the buyers come from?

From every channel serious buyers use — REA Premier Plus, Domain Platinum, Audience Maximiser, the Barry Plant buyer database, and targeted social media. We don't rely on one portal. We make sure every buyer looking in your area sees your home before they see the competition.

04
Can I trust the price advice you give me?

I'll only tell you what the market supports. Overquoting to win your business — then managing expectations down the track — doesn't serve you. You deserve honest advice upfront so you can plan with confidence, not be surprised later.

05
How will you manage the process?

You'll have one agent, start to finish. Every call returned. Every open home attended personally. Every buyer followed up the same day. I run the process — you stay informed, without having to chase anyone.
